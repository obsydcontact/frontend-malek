If you work in Lausanne in finance, consulting, or healthcare, you know this scenario: three business dinners this week, a flight to Zurich on Tuesday, and a calendar that leaves zero room for "cooking healthy every night." Most nutrition advice online completely ignores this reality.

## The problem isn't your discipline

Most professionals I coach aren't lacking discipline — they're lacking a system that accounts for their real life. A meal plan that requires weighing every meal at home collapses the first week of travel.

## What actually works

1. **A framework, not a list of bans.** Learning to structure a plate (protein, vegetables, quality carbs) works in any restaurant, in Lausanne or anywhere else.
2. **Pre-calculated flexibility.** If you know a business dinner is coming Thursday, you adjust the rest of the week — not the other way around.
3. **Weekly tracking, not daily.** Day-to-day fluctuations mean nothing. The 7-day trend does.

## What this actually changes

My Lausanne clients lose an average of 0.5–1kg of fat per week without ever having to cancel a work dinner. It's not a miracle method — it's simply a method built for your reality, not for an influencer who works from home.
