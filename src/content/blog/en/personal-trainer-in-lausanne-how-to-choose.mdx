Lausanne has many personal trainers, and quality varies enormously. Here are the questions to ask before committing.

## 1. What are their real certifications?

A serious coach can name their certifications precisely (NASM, sports nutrition, etc.) rather than simply saying "I'm certified."

## 2. How does follow-up work between sessions?

Good coaching isn't limited to the hour of the session. Ask how adjustments are made week after week, and based on what data.

## 3. Does the program adapt to your schedule, or the other way around?

If the coach forces a rigid format without accounting for your professional constraints, the odds of dropping out within 8 weeks are high.

## 4. What happens if you travel or something comes up?

A program that collapses the first week of business travel isn't a good program.

## 5. Can you speak to past clients?

Verifiable testimonials (with name, role, city) are worth more than any marketing promise.
